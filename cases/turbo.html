[ 02 Overview ]

Turbo.ua delivers groceries across Kyiv in 30 minutes. I designed the app and the website from zero, and after the release I built and led the creative team.

How it works

[ 04 steps ]
01Dark storesEvery district of Kyiv has its own dark store, stocked like the nearest shop.
02PickingA picker assembles the order, checks the quality of every product and hands it to a courier.
03Courier appCouriers get the exact address, comments and a GPS tracker. The client follows the delivery live.
04BikesCouriers ride bicycles and carry up to 12 kg per order.

[ Main goal ]

Easy to order. Hard to disappoint.

A 30-minute delivery service, as simple as possible from opening the app to receiving the order. I studied where competitors fail and designed against two failures: the product is out of stock, and the courier is late.
