Crypto payroll wallet · iOS · dark-only

Pap-Pay

Salary arrives in BTC. Cashing out to a card is the first move — and it can't be undone.

Context

You didn't choose crypto. It came with the contract.

Employees of a media holding are paid in BTC and convert to dollars by sending to their own card. Accounts and accruals come from an external system — the app doesn't own them. The first operation a user ever makes is spending their own salary, and it's irreversible: a card transfer can be disputed, a blockchain transaction can't. The job was to separate the two models of money so the difference reads before confirmation, not after.
